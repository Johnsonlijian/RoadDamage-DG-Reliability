# R59 Public Literature And Data Additions

Date checked: 2026-06-13

These sources are added as verified public context for the Automation in Construction acceptance-gate framing. They are not new experimental data for the detector; they support the engineering-decision logic that pavement condition evidence informs maintenance, funding, treatment, and quality-management decisions.

## Boundary

The added sources support the article's decision-workflow context. They do not prove detector generalization, do not change the R43 K-grid result, and do not justify a universal deployment threshold.
